# Research and Retrieval Workload

This workload complements support triage by stressing context control and evidence quality.

## Why this workload matters

It exercises:

- retrieval quality
- context pruning
- evidence-grounded outputs
- memory compaction
- output validation for claims and citations

## Early benchmark ideas

- retrieve only relevant context
- avoid unnecessary context expansion
- produce outputs grounded in supplied evidence
- fail safely when evidence is insufficient
